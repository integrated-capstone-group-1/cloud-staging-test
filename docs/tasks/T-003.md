## 2. In-Scope Contract Slice
* Implement internal endpoint: `POST /internal/risk/evaluate` as specified in [`docs/CONTRACTS.md`](file:///c:/Users/RBS83790/OneDrive%20-%20EastWest%20Banking%20Corporation/Documents/training/integrated-capstone/capstone-banking-system/docs/CONTRACTS.md):
  ```json
  {
    "transaction_id": "txn-5541-uuid",
    "customer_id": "cust-user-001",
    "amount": "2517.2000",
    "currency": "PHP",
    "ip_address": "192.168.1.50",
    "device_id": "dev-fingerprint-99"
  }
  ```
* Calculate normalized weighted risk score $S \in [0.00, 1.00]$ (Resolving B-05):
  $$S = \min\left(1.00, \; 0.40 \cdot V + 0.35 \cdot A + 0.25 \cdot D\right)$$
  * $V \in \{0.0, 1.0\}$: Velocity ($> 3$ transfers in 5 minutes $\implies V=1.0$).
  * $A \in \{0.0, 1.0\}$: Amount Anomaly ($> 100,000$ PHP or just under AMLA threshold $\implies A=1.0$).
  * $D \in \{0.0, 1.0\}$: Device Anomaly (New or unverified device $\implies D=1.0$).
* Calibrated Decision Mapping (ADR-001):
  * $S < 0.50 \implies \text{LOW} \implies \text{ALLOW}$
  * $0.50 \le S \le 0.85 \implies \text{MEDIUM} \implies \text{STEP\_UP\_OTP}$
  * $S > 0.85 \implies \text{HIGH} \implies \text{HOLD}$ (Triggers when $V=1, A=1, D=1 \implies S = 1.00 > 0.85$, setting status `RESERVED` and enqueuing in admin held review)
* Caller SLA Timeout Enforcement:
  * Resilience4j `TimeLimiter` set to $\le 200\text{ms}$.
  * On timeout or error, caller must fail closed (`HTTP 503 SECURITY_CHECK_UNAVAILABLE`).
